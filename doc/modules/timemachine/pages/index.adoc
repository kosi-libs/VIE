= TimeMachine

TimeMachine is a live inspector for VIE ViewModels.
A _probe_ in your application streams every Model, Intent and Signal of the ViewModels it observes to the _inspector_, a desktop app that shows them as a timeline per ViewModel.
The inspector can also send an object back into a live ViewModel, to drive it into a state that is hard to reach by hand.

IMPORTANT: TimeMachine is not published yet.
Its modules build from a checkout of VIE, where the task-list demo in `demo/` has the probe wired in.


[#scope]
== What it does

TimeMachine observes and intervenes.
It does not rewind, restore snapshots or persist anything: the inspector keeps what it hears in memory, and starts empty again when it restarts.

A session is one run of your application.
The inspector lists the sessions it has heard from, the ViewModels of each, and for each ViewModel the Models, Intents and Signals it went through, in order.


[#modules]
== Modules

[cols="2,1,4"]
|===
| Module | Role | Holds

| `vie-timemachine-client`
| Probe
| The library your application embeds, for every platform VIE supports.
It observes one ViewModel per middleware, keeps one WebSocket per application process, and applies what the inspector sends.

| `timemachine-server`
| Inspector
| A Compose Desktop application.
It runs the WebSocket server, keeps the sessions, ViewModels and timelines in memory, and shows them.

| `vie-timemachine-shared`
| Contract
| The wire messages, the JSON configuration, the connection coordinates and the protocol version, which both other modules depend on.
|===

The module names follow the WebSocket roles: your application is the WebSocket client, and the inspector is its server.
The pages say probe and inspector.

TIP: To add the probe to your application, see xref:setup.adoc[].
